# Art Curation & Gallery Walls: How to Collect, Frame, and Display Art

Art transforms a decorated house into a personal home with soul, narrative, and emotional resonance.

---

## 1. Scale and Proportions
A common mistake is hanging artwork that is too small for the wall. Above a sofa or sideboard, art should occupy roughly two-thirds (60% to 75%) of the furniture width.

## 2. The Universal Hanging Height
Museum standard dictates that the vertical center of an artwork should hang at **57 to 60 inches (145 to 152 cm)** from the finished floor, aligning with the human eye level.

## 3. Conservation Framing
Always request acid-free museum rag mat boards and UV-filtering non-reflective glass to preserve fine art photography and original works on paper across generations.
