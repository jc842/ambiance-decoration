# Color Harmonies: Earthy Terracotta, Sunlit Sand, and Ochre Palettes

Earth-inspired tones ground interior spaces in natural warmth, evoking Mediterranean villas and desert retreats.

---

## 1. The 60-30-10 Composition Rule
- **60% Dominant Base**: Soft chalky beige, warm linen, or pale limestone on walls and major floor coverings.
- **30% Secondary Body**: Rich terracotta, warm hazelnut wood, or raw clay on large upholstery and cabinetry.
- **10% Accent Accent**: Deep sienna, burnished brass, or dark olive velvet cushions and ceramic vases.

## 2. Light Interaction with Earth Pigments
Mineral-based earth pigments react dynamically to the sun's trajectory throughout the day, shifting from crisp ochre at noon to comforting amber glow at twilight.
