# Architectural Lighting: Mastering Ambient, Task, and Accent Illumination

Lighting defines the mood, perceived volume, and intimacy of any interior space. A successful lighting scheme avoids single glaring ceiling fixtures in favor of multi-layered illumination.

---

## 1. The Three Fundamental Layers
1. **Ambient Lighting**: Soft, indirect overall illumination, typically provided by concealed plaster coves or upwards-facing sconces.
2. **Task Lighting**: Focused, glare-free light directed at functional zones (kitchen prep islands, reading nooks, bathroom vanities).
3. **Accent Lighting**: Precise spotlighting (narrow 15°–24° beam angles) highlighting artworks, stone feature walls, and bespoke niches.

## 2. Color Temperature (Kelvin Scale)
For domestic relaxation, aim strictly for **2700K (Warm White)** or **2400K (Candlelight Amber)**. Avoid sterile 4000K+ clinical cool whites in living areas.

## 3. Dimmability & Scene Controls
Installing multi-channel dimmers allows you to transition your space effortlessly from daytime clarity to intimate evening lounge ambiance.
