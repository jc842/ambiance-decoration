# Biophilic Home Decor: Bringing Nature Indoors for Mental Wellbeing

Biophilic design satisfies our innate evolutionary need to connect with nature, improving mood, cognitive focus, and overall vitality within the home.

---

## 1. Direct Nature: Specimen Indoor Botanicals
Choose sculptural plants suited to indoor humidity and light levels, such as *Ficus lyrata*, *Monstera deliciosa*, and *Strelitzia nicolai*. Grouping plants creates lush micro-climates.

## 2. Acoustic Wood Slat Paneling
FSC-certified oak and walnut acoustic slat panels backed with recycled acoustic felt absorb sound echoes while infusing spaces with forest aesthetics.

## 3. Water and Air Quality
Incorporating natural stone indoor water features and non-toxic mineral finishes ensures indoor air remains free of volatile organic compounds (VOCs).
